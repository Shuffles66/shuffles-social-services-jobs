## 1. Purpose and scope
This policy sets out how we make and publish advertising and media. It covers all advertising, marketing, promotional content and commercial media that uses the platform or its brand. It also covers any image or video a member adds to a listing or profile. The goal is simple: our marketing must be respectful, honest, safe and fair to every person it touches, especially people with disability and participants.

## 2. Our principles
All of our advertising and media is:
- **Respectful**: it upholds the dignity of every person shown.
- **Person-centred**: it reflects real people and real choices, not stereotypes.
- **Strengths-based**: it shows ability and contribution, not pity or fear.
- **Honest**: it is truthful and never overstates what we do.
- **Never exploitative**: no person is used, pressured or taken advantage of for content.

These principles align to the **NDIS Code of Conduct**. Participant dignity and privacy come first, ahead of any marketing goal.

## 3. Children in advertising and media
**Our default is that we prefer not to use children in advertising or commercial media.** Wherever possible we tell our story without depicting children.

If a child must appear, strict safeguards apply:
- **Written consent** from a parent or legal guardian before any filming, photography or publishing.
- **Worker screening**: every adult involved in the production holds the relevant screening and a current **Working With Children Check** (or the equivalent in their state or territory).
- **Child-safe practices** throughout, including a supervising adult present at all times.
- **No content that could identify a vulnerable child or place them at risk** (no full name, no home, school or location details, no information that could enable contact).
- **Compliance with child-protection law** and the advertising codes that apply to children.

## 4. Fair pay for commercial media production
Anyone who appears in, or contributes to, media made for commercial purposes must be paid fairly. This includes people with disability, participants, workers and carers. Payment is at fair market rates for their time and contribution.
- **No one is asked to work for free** or "for exposure".
- **Reasonable adjustments** are provided so people can take part on equal terms.
- **Accessible production practices** are used (accessible venues, breaks, plain instructions, support persons welcome).

## 5. Informed consent
Informed consent is central to this policy. We obtain **written, informed consent** from every person who appears (or from their guardian or nominee) **before publishing**.

Consent must be genuine, freely given, specific and documented. It must cover:
- **How the content will be used** (the purpose).
- **On which channels** it will appear.
- **For how long** it may be used.
- **The right to withdraw consent**, and what happens to the content when someone withdraws.
- **Any remuneration that has been agreed**: the agreed payment is recorded as part of the consent.

No person is pressured to consent, and no service or opportunity depends on consenting.

## 6. Image and footage rights, storage and withdrawal
- We use images and footage only for the purposes set out in the signed consent.
- Original files and consent records are stored securely, with access limited to those who need it.
- A person (or their guardian or nominee) can **withdraw consent at any time**. On withdrawal we stop using the content in any new material and remove it from channels we control as soon as practicable. We explain that copies already shared or printed by others may not be fully recoverable.
- Files are kept only as long as needed and then securely destroyed in line with our retention schedule.

## 7. Truthful advertising
- No **false, misleading or deceptive** claims about our services, results or status.
- No implying **endorsement, registration or outcomes** without evidence and the consent of anyone named.
- **No naming or identifying a participant** in advertising or promotional media.
- Testimonials and statistics are real, current and able to be supported if questioned.

## 8. Accessibility and inclusive representation
- **Captions** on video, and, where practical, **audio description**.
- Plain-language text and good colour contrast in graphics.
- **Respectful, diverse and non-tokenistic representation** across disability, CALD, First Nations and LGBTQIA+ communities.
- People are shown as participants in everyday life, not as objects of inspiration or sympathy.

## 9. Privacy
- **Participant anonymity is never traded for marketing.** A more compelling story never justifies exposing a participant.
- We collect and use only the personal information needed for the agreed content.
- For how we handle personal information generally, see our **Privacy Policy** at {{link to Privacy Policy}}.

## 10. Breaches and enforcement
If advertising or media breaches this policy we may:
- **Remove or take down** the content.
- **Suspend or cancel** the responsible member's ability to publish.
- **Refer the matter** to a regulator, screening authority, or the police where required (for example, a child-safety concern).

We treat any concern about a child's safety, or about a participant being exploited, as urgent.

### Plain-English summary (for participants)
*Our ads and videos must be honest, respectful and safe. We prefer not to show children, and if we ever do there are strict rules to keep them safe. If you take part in a video or photo for our marketing, we pay you fairly, we never ask you to do it for free, and we ask you to sign a consent form first that explains how it will be used and how you can say "stop" later. We never use your name or your story without your written permission, and your privacy always comes before our marketing.*

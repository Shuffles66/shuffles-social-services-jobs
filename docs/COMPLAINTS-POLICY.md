## 1. Purpose
We welcome complaints and feedback. They help us put things right and improve our service. This policy explains how anyone can make a complaint, how we handle it fairly and promptly, and your right to take a complaint to the NDIS Quality and Safeguards Commission at any time.

## 2. Our principles
Every complaint is handled in a way that is:
- **Accessible** — easy to make, in the format and language that suits you (including anonymously).
- **Person-centred & respectful** — you are listened to, treated with dignity, and supported to take part.
- **Fair** — procedural fairness for everyone involved; decisions are impartial and based on the facts.
- **Responsive** — acknowledged quickly and resolved as soon as practicable.
- **Safe** — **no person will be adversely affected** for making a complaint (no retaliation, no reduction in service).
- **Confidential** — information is handled per privacy law and shared only as needed to resolve the matter or where the law requires.

## 3. What is a complaint
A complaint is any expression of dissatisfaction about our services, a worker or provider, safety, conduct, or how a previous complaint was handled. You do **not** need to use the word "complaint".

## 4. Who can complain
Anyone — a participant, a family member, carer, nominee or **advocate** acting on someone's behalf, a worker, a contractor, a provider, or a member of the public. You can ask a support person or advocate to help you at any stage, and you can complain **anonymously**.

- **Email:** {{complaints@…}}

This gives a single, auditable complaints register.

## 6. How we handle your complaint
1. **Acknowledge** — we acknowledge every complaint promptly, normally **within 2 business days** (anonymous complaints are still recorded and actioned).
2. **Record** — the complaint is logged in our complaints register (Fluent Support) with the date, details, and your preferred contact method.
3. **Assess & triage** — we decide the seriousness and whether it must be escalated (e.g. a **reportable incident** or an immediate risk to a person's safety — these follow our separate Incident Management process and are notified to the NDIS Commission as required).
4. **Resolve** — we work with you (and the people involved) to resolve the matter **as quickly as practicable**, keeping you informed of progress and likely timeframes. We aim to resolve most complaints **within 21 days**; if it will take longer we tell you why and when to expect an update.
5. **Outcome** — we tell you the outcome, the reasons, and any action taken or changes made. If you're not satisfied, you can ask for an internal review or take the matter to the NDIS Commission (below).
6. **Improve** — outcomes and trends are reviewed by management to prevent recurrence and improve our service.

## 7. Your right to contact the NDIS Commission
You can contact the **NDIS Quality and Safeguards Commission at any time** — you do not have to come to us first:
- **Phone:** 1800 035 544 (free from landlines) · **TTY:** 133 677
- **Online:** ndiscommission.gov.au
- **Interpreters** available on request.

You may also contact other relevant bodies (e.g. the Aged Care Quality and Safety Commission for aged-care matters, the relevant state/territory complaints body, Fair Work for employment matters, or the police where a crime may have occurred).

## 8. No detriment
No participant, worker or provider will be treated unfairly, lose services, or be disadvantaged in any way for making a complaint in good faith, supporting a complaint, or contacting the NDIS Commission.

## 9. Privacy and records
We collect and use only the information needed to manage the complaint, in line with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and our Privacy Policy. Complaint records are stored securely in Fluent Support, kept for the period required by law/our retention schedule, and access is limited to those handling the matter.

## 10. Roles and responsibilities
- **All staff/representatives** — receive complaints respectfully, record them, and pass them to the Complaints Officer.
- **Complaints Officer ({{name/role}})** — manages the register, ensures acknowledgement and timeframes, oversees fair handling, and reports trends to management.
- **Management** — ensures resources, reviews trends, drives improvement, and ensures Commission notifications where required.

## 11. Monitoring and continuous improvement
We review complaint data at least {{quarterly}} to identify themes and improvement actions, and we review this policy at least **annually** and after any significant incident or regulatory change.

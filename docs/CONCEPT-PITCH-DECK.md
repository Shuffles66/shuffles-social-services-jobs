# Concept Pitch Deck — Shuffles Social Services Jobs and Engagements (outline)

*Slide-by-slide outline for a partner / investor concept deck. Each slide lists the headline, the body beats, and a presenter note. Build in Canva/Slides from this; copy is drawn from `EXEC-SUMMARY.md` and `FEATURES.md`.*

---

### Slide 1 — Title
- **Shuffles Social Services Jobs and Engagements** — *The work marketplace built for disability, aged care and social services.*
- Sub: four-sided · ABN/TFN-aware · participant-safe · accessible by default.
- Presenter note: one breath — "It's a jobs board that finally understands this sector."

### Slide 2 — The problem
- Providers can't find checked, available workers.
- Participants who self-/plan-manage have **nowhere safe** to find their own workers.
- Generic boards mix up contractor vs employee work, assume credentials, expose vulnerable people, and aren't accessible.
- Presenter note: land the participant-safety gap hard — it's the emotional core.

### Slide 3 — Why now
- NDIS self-/plan-management is large and growing → a real direct-hire market with no home.
- Workforce shortage across disability + aged care.
- Rising compliance + accessibility expectations.

### Slide 4 — The idea
- A **four-sided** marketplace: employers/agencies ↔ workers, **and** participants ↔ workers.
- Diagram: four boxes, two axes.
- Presenter note: most boards have one axis; the second is the whole point.

### Slide 5 — Differentiator 1: ABN vs TFN, kept apart
- Contractor (ABN) work and employee (TFN) work on **separate boards**, enforced at the data layer.
- Participant board = ABN-only; TFN board never shows ABN.
- ABN recorded + verified against the national register.

### Slide 6 — Differentiator 2: participants anonymous by design
- No real name · suburb-only · no public contact · safe internal relay · hidden from Google · human-moderated.
- Presenter note: "Privacy beats discoverability — always."

### Slide 7 — Differentiator 3: verified, pre-matched workers
- WWCC / Blue Card, NDIS Worker Screening, police checks, First Aid, insurance — **admin-verified**, not self-claimed.
- Matches only surface workers already cleared for that job.

### Slide 8 — Differentiator 4: accessible + multilingual, $0 to run
- Voice search, 7 languages (Arabic RTL), read-aloud, high-contrast / no-colour / larger-text / Easy-Read.
- Browser-side — no per-use cost.

### Slide 9 — Differentiator 5: found by Google
- `JobPosting` structured data → Google for Jobs eligible; indexable job + category pages; sitemaps.
- Private participant listings stay `noindex`.

### Slide 10 — Flexible funding
- One, many, or no funding source: NDIS, aged care, DVA, iCare, Medicare, private, none.
- Not locked to NDIS → whole-of-sector + future-proof.

### Slide 11 — How it works (flow)
- Post (job / worker / need) → verify → match (location + credentials + availability) → safe contact → hire.
- Simple 5-step strip.

### Slide 12 — Business model
- Three streams: **employer advertising subscription**, **provider application-fee subscription**, **resell licence** (white-label per sector).
- Low cost to operate (shared maps key, shared AI engine, $0 accessibility).

### Slide 13 — Market
- Disability (NDIS), aged care, allied health, youth + community services.
- Sector-agnostic core → expandable to new sectors and regions via white-label.

### Slide 14 — Built on a trusted stack
- Same platform + AI matching engine as Shuffles Provider Finder; integrates PMPro, FluentCart, FluentCRM, Maps.
- Internal listings surface inside members' existing referral research — not another silo.

### Slide 15 — Traction / status
- Design locked (v1.1); MVP (Phases 0–3) ships the ABN/TFN boards, accessibility and SEO together.
- Founding-partner program open for seeding.

### Slide 16 — Safeguards & endorsement
- Australian Privacy Principles, NDIS Code of Conduct, human moderation, flag-only banned-ABN cross-match.
- Seeking concept endorsements from peak bodies.

### Slide 17 — The ask
- Founding partners to seed listings + co-design.
- Endorsers for the concept.
- *(If investor audience)* the funding ask + use of funds.

### Slide 18 — Close
- *"A safer, smarter way to connect work and workers in the sectors that need it most."*
- Contact + provider.directory.

# Endorse the Concept — Shuffles Social Services Jobs and Engagements

**One page · For peak bodies, advocacy organisations and sector leaders**

---

## The idea, in three sentences

The care-and-support sector needs a work marketplace built for *its* realities — not a generic job board. Shuffles Social Services Jobs and Engagements separates contractor (ABN) work from employee (TFN) work, lets participants find their own workers **anonymously and safely**, verifies worker credentials before matching, and is **accessible and multilingual** by default. We're seeking endorsements of the **concept** from organisations who care about safety, dignity and choice in this sector.

## Why it deserves support

- **Choice and control.** Self- and plan-managed participants finally get a safe, dignified place to find their own workers — advancing the core promise of the NDIS.
- **Safety first.** Participant anonymity is built into the architecture, not bolted on. Worker checks are verified by people, not self-claimed.
- **Inclusion by default.** Voice search, seven languages, read-aloud and Easy-Read come standard — at no running cost — so the people the sector serves aren't shut out.
- **Doing it properly.** ABN/TFN done correctly, credentials verified, privacy law respected, moderation by humans.

## What an endorsement means

You are endorsing the **concept and its principles** — not auditing code or guaranteeing outcomes. Concretely, you might:

- Allow us to list your organisation as a **concept endorser** (logo + a short quote).
- Provide a **statement of support** we can share with other partners and funders.
- *(Optional)* Offer **guidance** on standards we should meet (credentialing, accessibility, participant safeguards).

## What you get

- Recognition as an early supporter of a safer model for sector hiring.
- A voice in the safeguards and standards baked into the product.
- Early visibility of the platform for your members/community.
- No cost, no obligation, no exclusivity.

## Our commitments to endorsers

- We will **name our principles publicly** (anonymity, verified-not-assumed, accessibility, ABN/TFN correctness) and hold the product to them.
- We will **never** use an endorsement to imply you audited or are liable for the platform.
- We will **share progress** honestly, including what isn't built yet.

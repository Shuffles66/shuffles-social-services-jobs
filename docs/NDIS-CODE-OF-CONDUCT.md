## 1. Purpose
This policy sets out the standards of behaviour we expect from every worker, contractor and provider who uses the platform to offer or deliver supports to people with disability. It adopts the **seven elements of the NDIS Code of Conduct** as our minimum standard, explains what they mean in practice on our platform, and makes clear how we respond when they are not met.

## 2. Who it applies to
The Code applies to **all workers, contractors and providers** — registered or unregistered, paid or volunteer — who use the platform to advertise, find, offer or deliver NDIS supports or services to participants. By creating a profile or responding to a listing, you agree to comply with the Code at all times when delivering or arranging supports.

## 3. The seven elements of the NDIS Code of Conduct
Workers and providers must, in providing supports and services to people with disability:
1. **Act with respect for individual rights to freedom of expression, self-determination and decision-making**, in accordance with applicable laws and conventions.
2. **Respect the privacy** of people with disability.
3. **Provide supports and services in a safe and competent manner, with care and skill.**
4. **Act with integrity, honesty and transparency.**
5. **Promptly take steps to raise and act on concerns** about matters that may affect the quality and safety of supports and services provided to people with disability.
6. **Take all reasonable steps to prevent and respond to all forms of violence against, and exploitation, neglect and abuse of, people with disability.**
7. **Take all reasonable steps to prevent and respond to sexual misconduct.**

## 4. What this means on our platform
- **Honest listings.** Describe your services, qualifications, availability and pricing truthfully. No misrepresentation of identity, registration status, experience or credentials.
- **Respect participant privacy and pseudonymity.** Participant needs are pseudonymous by design — do not attempt to identify, locate or contact a participant outside the platform's relay, and never share their information.
- **Use the relay.** First contact and sensitive communication go through the platform's internal messaging relay so safeguards (records, moderation, reporting) stay intact.
- **No soliciting off-platform to avoid safeguards.** Do not push participants or workers to move the engagement off-platform in order to bypass screening, records, complaints or incident processes.
- **Keep credentials current.** Maintain valid screening, checks and qualifications (e.g. NDIS Worker Screening, WWCC, police checks, insurances) and update them on your profile as they renew or lapse.

## 5. Acknowledgement
All workers, contractors and providers must **acknowledge this Code at onboarding** and keep that acknowledgement visible on their profile. The acknowledgement is surfaced in-product — at sign-up/onboarding and on the profile — so it is recorded, dated and renewable. Failing to acknowledge, or withdrawing acknowledgement, means you may not offer or deliver supports through the platform.

## 6. Breaches and consequences
Where we reasonably believe the Code has been breached, and depending on the seriousness, we may: issue a **warning**, require corrective action, **suspend** a profile or its ability to respond to listings, or **remove** the worker, contractor or provider from the platform. Serious breaches — particularly those involving harm, violence, exploitation, neglect, abuse or sexual misconduct — may be **referred to the NDIS Quality and Safeguards Commission** and, where a crime may have occurred, to the **police**. Action is taken fairly, with reasons given, while prioritising the safety of participants.

## 7. Reporting concerns
If you have a concern about the quality or safety of supports, or about conduct under this Code, **raise it promptly**:
- **Internally:** use our complaints process — see the **Complaints Management and Resolution Policy** (online Support form, email or phone). Concerns can be raised confidentially and, where appropriate, anonymously.
- **NDIS Quality and Safeguards Commission** (you can contact them at any time): **Phone 1800 035 544** (free from landlines) · **TTY 133 677** · **ndiscommission.gov.au**.

No person will be disadvantaged for raising a concern in good faith. Where there is an immediate risk to a person's safety, contact emergency services on **000** first.

### Plain-English summary (for participants)
*Everyone who offers or delivers supports through this platform must follow the NDIS Code of Conduct. That means they treat you with respect, keep your information private, work safely and skilfully, are honest, speak up about anything unsafe, and never harm, exploit, neglect, abuse or behave inappropriately toward you. If something isn't right, you can tell us, or call the NDIS Commission any time on **1800 035 544** — and in an emergency, call **000**.*

# Founding Partner Prospectus — Shuffles Social Services Jobs and Engagements

**A safer, smarter way to connect work and workers across disability, aged care and social services.**

*Version 1.1 · 5 June 2026 · For prospective founding partners — providers, agencies, plan managers and peak bodies.*

## The invitation

We are building the Shuffles Social Services Jobs and Engagements: a sector-specific work marketplace that finally treats the realities of care-and-support hiring as first-class — the **ABN vs TFN** distinction, **participant-led direct hire**, **verified credentials**, and **accessibility** for the communities this sector serves.

We're inviting a small group of **founding partners** to shape it with us and to seed it with real listings before public launch. Founding partners get preferential terms, a direct line into the product roadmap, and first-mover visibility in their region or specialty.

---

## Why this, why now

- **The workforce crisis is real.** Providers can't find checked, available workers; participants who self- or plan-manage have nowhere safe to advertise for one.
- **Generic boards don't fit.** They mix up contractor and employee work, assume credentials rather than verifying them, and expose vulnerable people's details.
- **The direct-hire market is invisible.** Self- and plan-managed participants are a large and growing slice of the sector, and they have almost no safe, dignified place to find their own workers.

Shuffles Social Services Jobs and Engagements is purpose-built for exactly these gaps.

## What makes it different

| Problem with generic boards | What we do |
|---|---|
| Contractor and employee jobs mixed together | **Strictly separated ABN and TFN boards** — never confused |
| Participants exposed when seeking workers | **Anonymous by design** — no real name, suburb-only, contact via safe relay, hidden from Google |
| "Verified" means "they ticked a box" | **Admin-verified** WWCC / NDIS screening / police / insurance — and matches are **pre-checked** |
| Locked to one funding type | **Any funding, or none** — NDIS, aged care, DVA, iCare, Medicare, private |
| Inaccessible to CALD and disabled users | **Voice search, 7 languages, read-aloud, Easy-Read** — at no extra cost |
| Buried in search | **Google-for-Jobs-ready** job pages (while private listings stay private) |

## What we're asking of founding partners

Pick what fits — none of this is onerous:

1. **Seed listings.** Post a handful of your real open roles (and, where relevant, encourage self-managing participants you support to post a need) so the board launches with genuine activity.

3. **A testimonial or case story** once you've seen it work.
4. *(Optional)* **Endorse the concept** publicly — see the separate Endorsement one-pager.

## How it protects everyone

- **Participants:** anonymous, moderated, contact-gated, never indexed.
- **Workers:** verified once, surfaced only for roles they're already cleared for; documents stored privately and purged on schedule.
- **Providers:** real, checkable business identities (ABN verified against the national register); a clean hiring pipeline.
- **Everyone:** Australian Privacy Principles, NDIS Code of Conduct acknowledgement, human moderation of sensitive listings.

## The model (so there are no surprises)

- **Employers/agencies:** post a job free to start; subscribe to post more or to feature listings.
- **Providers/workers:** subscribe to respond to participant needs and ABN tasks.
- **Operators:** the platform can be **licensed and white-labelled** for other sectors and regions.

Founding partners receive preferential terms across all of the above during the founding period.

## 1. Purpose and scope
We respect your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it, how we use and protect it, and the choices and rights you have. It covers the **{{Just Tasks}}** website, the jobs and engagements marketplace, and any related services we run. By using the platform you agree to this policy.

## 2. What personal information we collect
We collect different information depending on who you are:
- **Participants (NDIS / aged care).** Participants are **pseudonymous**. We store a **pseudonym code** and a **suburb** only. A participant's real name and contact details are **never shown publicly** and are never part of a public listing. Needs may include support categories, hours, schedule and preferences — kept general, never identifying.
- **Workers, contractors and sole traders.** Profile details (name or display name, services offered, location, availability, rate, experience, languages, qualifications and credentials, and résumés you upload).
- **Organisations (advertisers).** Business name, **ABN**, organisation type, and contact people (names, email, phone, role).
- **Account and usage data.** Login details, membership/tier, the listings and messages you create, and technical data such as IP address, device/browser type and pages visited.

## 3. How we collect it
We collect information in these ways:
- **Directly from you** — when you register, create or edit a profile or listing, upload documents, apply or respond, or contact us.
- **Through your account** — as you use the platform (saved searches, applications, messages).
- **Automatically** — through cookies and similar technologies (see section 12) and standard web server logs.

## 4. Why we use it
We use personal information to run the marketplace and keep people safe, including to:
- **Match** workers, organisations and participant needs.
- **Relay messages** between members through our internal messaging (first contact never exposes your details).
- **Verify** credentials, ABNs and registrations, and review listings.
- **Send alerts and service messages** you have asked for (e.g. new matches, expiry reminders).
- **Maintain safety, security and integrity** — moderation, fraud prevention, and meeting our legal obligations.

## 5. Sensitive information
Some information is **sensitive** under the Privacy Act — for example health or disability information, and criminal-record checks obtained through worker screening (such as police checks or a Working with Children Check). We collect sensitive information **only with your consent** and **only for the stated purpose** (matching, verification and safety). You can decline, but some features may then be unavailable. We do not use sensitive information for any other purpose without your consent or where the law requires it.

## 6. Disclosure
- **Internal relay first.** First contact between members goes through our internal messaging. Contact details (email, phone) are **not shared between members** unless and until a member chooses to share them.
- **Service providers.** We use trusted providers to operate the platform (hosting, email delivery, payments). They may handle personal information only to provide their service to us and are bound to protect it.
- **Internal search vendor.** We use a third-party AI/search vendor **internally** to help with matching and discovery. This vendor is **never named to members**, and **participant data is never exposed to it**.
- **When the law requires.** We may disclose information where required or authorised by law, to protect someone's safety, or to a regulator (e.g. the NDIS Quality and Safeguards Commission) where obliged.

## 7. Credential and document storage
Credential documents and résumés (e.g. police checks, qualification scans, CVs) are stored **privately in our database** with **no public link**. They are **served only to the owner and authorised administrators** through an access-checked path — never published on a profile or listing. They are retained in line with our **Data Retention Policy** and then securely deleted.

## 8. Storage and security
We take reasonable steps to protect personal information from misuse, loss, and unauthorised access, modification or disclosure — including access controls, capability checks, secure transmission (HTTPS), and restricting staff access to a need-to-know basis. No system is perfectly secure; if a notifiable data breach occurs we will respond under the **Notifiable Data Breaches** scheme and notify affected people and the OAIC as required.

## 10. Access and correction
You can review and update most of your information at any time by editing your profile or listing. Under the Privacy Act you may also **request access** to the personal information we hold about you and **ask us to correct** it. Contact us (section 15) and we will respond within a reasonable time. We may need to verify your identity first.

## 11. Data export and erasure
We support WordPress's built-in privacy tools — an administrator can use **Tools → Export Personal Data** and **Tools → Erase Personal Data** to action a request tied to your account email. Plugin-specific exporters/erasers for marketplace data (profiles, applications, messages) are being added (`class-privacy.php`). Either way you can export or erase your personal information on request, in line with this policy and our legal obligations. Some records may be retained where the law requires (e.g. financial or safety records).

## 12. Cookies and consent
We use cookies and similar technologies to keep you logged in, remember preferences, and understand how the platform is used. We aim for **privacy-preserving defaults** and seek consent for non-essential cookies. You can control cookies through your browser. For detail, see our **Cookie / Consent Policy**.

## 13. Complaints about privacy
If you think we have mishandled your personal information, please tell us first (section 15) — we will investigate and respond. If you are not satisfied, you can contact the **Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC)**:
- **Web:** oaic.gov.au · **Phone:** 1300 363 992

# The Rules — Shuffles Social Services Jobs and Engagements

**Plain-language guide · Version 1.1 · 5 June 2026**

This page explains how the Shuffles Social Services Jobs and Engagements works and the rules everyone follows.
It is written in short, simple sentences.
An **Easy Read** version with pictures can be made from this page if you need it.

---

## What this is

Shuffles Social Services Jobs and Engagements helps people find work and find workers in:

- disability support (NDIS)
- aged care
- allied health
- other community and social services

It connects **four** kinds of people:

1. **Employers and agencies** — they post jobs.
2. **Workers** — they look for work. We sometimes call them candidates.
3. **Participants** — people who need support and want to find a worker themselves.
4. **People who already work** but want more work, extra shifts, or a new ongoing role.

---

## The two kinds of work: ABN and TFN

There are two ways you can be paid for work. They are kept in **separate sections**.

### ABN work (you run a business)

- You have an **ABN** (Australian Business Number).
- You send an **invoice** to get paid.
- This includes sub-contracting and doing **tasks directly for a participant** (for example, NDIS work).
- **You must give your ABN.** Recording the ABN is very important.

### TFN work (you are an employee)

- You are **employed** by an employer.
- You give a **TFN** (Tax File Number). The employer takes out tax and pays super.
- You do **not** need an ABN for this work.

### The big rule about keeping them apart

- The **participant section** (participants looking for workers) shows **ABN work only**. It will **never** show TFN jobs.
- The **TFN section** shows employee jobs only. It will **never** show ABN listings.
- The **ABN section** shows business / contractor work only.

This keeps things clear, so no one applies for the wrong kind of work.

---

## Jobs can be one-off or ongoing

- A **one-off** job is a single task or a short engagement.
- An **ongoing** job is work that continues over time.

Employers say which kind it is when they post.

---

## Funding — you can pick one, many, or none

A participant can choose how their support is paid for. You can pick:

- NDIS
- Aged Care (Home Care Package or CHSP)
- DVA (Veterans)
- iCare / Workers' Compensation
- Medicare / Chronic Disease Management
- Privately funded (you pay yourself)
- **No funding** (none of the above)

You can pick **one**, **more than one**, or **none**. It is your choice.
This system is **not just for NDIS**.

---

## Keeping participants private and safe

This is one of our most important rules.

- A participant's **real name is never shown.** We use a private code instead.
- We only show the **suburb**, never a full address.
- A participant's **phone and email are never on a public listing.**
- First contact goes through our **safe internal messaging.** No one gets your details until you choose to share them.
- A participant's listing is **checked by our team before it goes live.**
- Participant pages are **hidden from Google** on purpose, to protect privacy.

---

## Workers must be checked

Workers in this sector look after people who can be vulnerable. So we check important things.

Depending on the job, a worker may need:

- **NDIS Worker Screening Check**
- **Working With Children Check** (Blue Card in Queensland)
- **Police check**
- **First Aid** and other certificates
- **Insurance** (for ABN / contractor work)

Rules about checks:

- A **"✓ Verified"** badge only appears **after our team has checked the evidence.**
- You **cannot** mark yourself as verified. Only an admin can.
- We store check documents in a **private, locked place.** Only approved staff can open them.
- We **delete** check documents after you leave, plus a short grace period.
- We **remind** workers before a check or certificate runs out.

### Pre-matching

When we suggest a worker for a job, we check first that the worker **already has the checks that job needs.** This is what we mean by "pre-matched."

## Finding work near you

- You can search by **location** and set a **distance** (radius).
- A **map** shows where jobs or workers are.
- Telehealth / remote work shows up even if it is far away.

---

## Applying and responding

- Workers and members who are signed in can **apply for jobs.**
- Employers see who applied and can move them through stages (new, shortlisted, interview, offer).
- To **respond to a participant** or do an ABN task, a worker needs a recorded **ABN** and an active **application subscription** (see Money, below).

---

## Money — simple version

There are three separate things. They are not the same.

1. **For employers — advertising subscription.**
   Posting one job can be free. To post many jobs, or to make a job stand out ("featured"), employers pay a subscription.

2. **For workers / providers — application subscription.**
   To **respond to participant jobs or ABN tasks**, a provider pays a subscription.

3. **For resellers — a product licence.**
   Other organisations can licence this whole system and run their own version (for example, for aged care).

You will always be told clearly before you pay anything.

## Built for everyone (accessibility)

The whole site can be changed to suit you. These tools are **free** to use:

- **Voice search** — search by speaking.
- **Other languages** — the screen can switch to Arabic, Mandarin, Greek, Italian, Indonesian or Punjabi (with more to come).
- **Read aloud** — the page can be read to you.
- **Display options** — high contrast, no colour, larger text, and Easy-Read mode.
- An **English** button is always there to switch back.

Your choices are remembered for next time.

---

## What is not allowed

To keep everyone safe, you must **not**:

- pretend to be someone else, or give false details
- post a participant's real name or private contact details
- claim a check or certificate you do not have
- use the messaging to harass, spam, or scam people
- try to view the wrong kind of work (we keep ABN and TFN apart on purpose)
- collect or share another person's information without their consent

Breaking these rules can mean your listing is removed or your account is suspended.

Everyone using the system agrees to act safely and respectfully.

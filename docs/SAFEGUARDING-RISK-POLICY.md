## 1. Purpose and our commitment
We connect people who need support with the workers and providers who deliver it. Because many of the people we serve are vulnerable, safeguarding is our first priority. We have **zero tolerance for violence, abuse, neglect, exploitation and discrimination** in any form. Every person on the platform has the right to be safe, to be treated with dignity, and to be free from harm. This policy explains how we prevent, identify and respond to risks to people's safety.

## 2. Who we protect
- **NDIS participants** and people receiving aged-care or other social-services support, including their families, carers, nominees and advocates.
- **Children and young people**, where they are involved in or affected by a support arrangement.
- **Other at-risk people** — anyone who may be at greater risk of harm because of disability, age, isolation, language, cultural background, or their circumstances.
We design for the most vulnerable user first; protections that keep participants safe protect everyone.

## 3. Worker screening as a safeguard
Verified credentials are a core safeguard. Depending on the role, workers may need a **Working with Children Check (WWCC) / Blue Card**, the **NDIS Worker Screening Check**, a **National Police Check**, and current **First Aid / CPR** qualifications.
- The public **✓ Verified** badge is shown **only after an administrator has checked the evidence** — it is **never self-claimed**. A worker ticking a box does not produce a badge.
- **Evidence is stored privately** (access-restricted, capability-gated, not exposed on any public listing) and is used solely to verify eligibility.
- **Expiry reminders** prompt workers and admins before a check lapses, so verified status reflects current credentials.
- These controls are **enforced in the product** via the credentials/verification system, not left to good intentions.

## 4. Platform safeguards by design
Safety is structural, so a single mistake cannot expose someone:
- **Participant pseudonymity** — participant listings never show a real name, and **location is suburb-only** (never a full address).
- **Internal relay messaging** — first contact goes through the platform; **contact details are not shared up front**, so people stay in control of who reaches them.
- **Pre-moderation of participant requests** — participant needs are **reviewed and approved by an admin before they go live**, never auto-published.
- **Moderated reviews** — testimonials and reviews are checked before publication to prevent abuse, coercion or retaliation.
- **No soliciting off-platform** — moving an engagement off the platform to avoid these safeguards is prohibited; doing so removes the protections that keep people safe.

## 5. Risk management approach
We manage safeguarding risk in a continuous cycle: **identify → assess → control → review.** Examples:
- **Impersonation / fake accounts** → admin verification, the ✓ Verified badge only after evidence is checked, and credential evidence stored privately.
- **Unsafe engagements** (unscreened worker, role mismatch, pressure tactics) → required-credential gating by role, segregated boards, and the ability to report and remove listings.
- **Privacy breaches** (exposing a participant's identity, address or contact details) → pseudonymity, suburb-only location, relay messaging, and access-controlled evidence storage.
- **Off-platform circumvention** → the no-soliciting rule plus monitoring for and acting on breaches.
Identified risks, their controls and any residual risk are recorded in a risk register and reviewed at least {{quarterly}} and after any significant incident or change.

## 6. Responding to safeguarding concerns
- **If someone is in immediate danger, call 000.**
- Report any safeguarding concern through our **Incident Management** process and, where it is a complaint, our **Complaints** process — see `docs/POLICIES.md`, `docs/COMPLAINTS-POLICY.md` and our incident-management procedure. We act on every concern; people who raise concerns in good faith are never disadvantaged.
- You can contact the **NDIS Quality and Safeguards Commission at any time** on **1800 035 544** (TTY 133 677, online at ndiscommission.gov.au) — you do not have to come to us first. For aged-care matters, contact the **Aged Care Quality and Safety Commission**; for a crime, contact the **police**.

## 7. Roles and responsibilities
- **All users** — treat others with respect, keep engagements on-platform, and report concerns promptly.
- **Workers and providers** — hold and maintain the required screening for their role, and never act outside their verified credentials.
- **Safeguarding Officer ({{name/role}})** — oversees verification, moderation, the risk register, and the response to safeguarding concerns; ensures Commission notifications where required.
- **Administrators / moderators** — verify credentials against evidence, moderate participant requests and reviews, and action reports.
- **Management** — resources safeguarding, reviews risk and incident trends, and drives continuous improvement.

### Plain-English summary (for participants)
*You have the right to be safe. We check workers' screening before showing a "✓ Verified" badge, we keep your name and address private, and first messages go through us so your contact details aren't shared until you choose. We don't allow people to take things off the platform to dodge these protections. If something doesn't feel right, tell us — and if you're ever in danger, call **000**. You can also call the NDIS Commission any time on **1800 035 544**.*
